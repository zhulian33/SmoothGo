# Presentation Guide

## Files

- `SmoothGo_Presentation.pptx` — editable 14-slide English deck with speaker notes.
- `SmoothGo_Presentation.pdf` — backup copy for rehearsal and submission.
- `rendered/slide-*.png` — quick preview images of every slide.

## Before you present

1. Replace all six `Member` placeholders on slide 1 with full names.
2. Open each slide’s speaker notes and adapt the script to your own voice.
3. Replace the phone mock-up on slide 5 with a screenshot or short recording of the real demo when it is ready.
4. Verify every reference on slide 14 through the SCU Library or the official source page.
5. Check that every number on slide 11 is labelled as an illustrative assumption.
6. Keep the presentation to 12–13 minutes, leaving 2–3 minutes for questions.

## Delivery rules

- Do not read the slides or a phone.
- Each slide should make one point.
- Use the “Replan” moment in the demo as the emotional peak.
- Say clearly that the prototype uses illustrative data.
- End with the pilot ask, not a generic thank-you.

## Suggested speaker order

1. Member B — opening and problem (slides 1–2)
2. Member C — solution, customers and competition (slides 3–4, 7)
3. Member A — product walkthrough and logic (slides 5–6)
4. Member D — business model and finance (slides 8, 11)
5. Member E — operations and marketing (slides 9–10)
6. Member F — challenges, conclusion and Q&A (slides 12–13)
